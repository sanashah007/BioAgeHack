Protocol aggressiveness

Each tier escalates in potency and risk. Cards show the markers driving your gap most, ranked by their weighted contribution. Coverage is partial — see the note below for which markers have a protocol yet.

Not a medical device. This reproduces a research pipeline on public NHANES data. A gap here is a population-level statistical association with mortality, not a diagnosis or a prediction about any individual. Reference curves use a healthy-reference population, so values are scored against people without the relevant diagnosed condition. Intervention entries are summarized from published literature with evidence grades and cover a subset of markers so far — a marker with no protocol card below simply has no entry yet, not a clean bill of health. Tier 3 (drugs) requires a prescription and physician supervision. Tier 4 (peptides) are largely not FDA-approved, carry limited human safety data, and are shown for information only. Consult a licensed clinician before changing diet, supplements, or medication.
