Educational tool — not a medical device, diagnosis, or treatment plan. This reproduces a research pipeline on public NHANES 2005–2006 data with linked mortality. A "gap" is a population-level statistical association with mortality, not a prediction about any individual. Reference curves use a healthy-reference population. Interventions are summarized from published literature with evidence grades; effects are general and may not apply to you. Tier 3 (drugs) requires a prescription and physician supervision. Tier 4 (peptides) are largely not FDA-approved, carry limited human safety data, and are shown for information only. Consult a licensed clinician before changing diet, supplements, or medication. Nothing here is uploaded — the entire calculation runs in your browser.
